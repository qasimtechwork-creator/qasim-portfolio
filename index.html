Questions

Before you ask.

How much does video editing cost?

$10–$15 per YouTube video, depending on length and complexity — captions, effects, sound design and color included. Thumbnails are $10 each for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Bigger jobs (brand packages, UGC ads, full websites) get a custom quote after a short brief.

How long does delivery take?

Most single videos and thumbnails are delivered within 1–3 days; larger packages depend on scope and are agreed before work starts. (Confirm typical timelines with Qasim when you order.) Need it faster? Ask about rush delivery.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes revisions until you're happy with the final cut — the goal is a video you're proud to publish, not a delivery count. (Confirm the revision policy for your package when you order.)

What do you need from me to start?

Just three things: 1) your raw footage or topic, 2) one or two reference videos you like, 3) your logo/brand colors if you have them. Everything else — script polish, visuals, captions, sound — is handled for you.

How does your AI video process work?

Concept first, then AI generation with tools like Google Flow and Veo, then a full professional edit in CapCut Pro — transitions, kinetic captions, sound effects and pacing tuned for retention. Your face, voice and brand stay exactly as they are; the AI builds around you, never over you.

Do you also design thumbnails?

Yes — thumbnail design is a core service at $10 per thumbnail for YouTube (16:9), Instagram (4:5) and TikTok (9:16). Every design respects the platform's safe zones so nothing important gets cropped, and text is kept short and clickable.
